About Us

Macroni Labs is an independent software development initiative focused on building useful, accessible, and thoughtfully designed technology.

We explore ideas across software engineering, automation, and artificial intelligence, turning them into practical tools that solve real-world problems.

Our approach is simple:

* Build with purpose — Focus on solving meaningful problems.
* Keep it accessible — Make useful technology available to everyone.
* Think independently — Experiment, innovate, and challenge conventional approaches.
* Build in the open — Support transparency and open-source collaboration.
